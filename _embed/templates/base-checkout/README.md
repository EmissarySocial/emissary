# Base Checkout

Shared base template (category: Base, model: Stream) that gives any Stream template a complete "sell this content" workflow: connecting a merchant account, attaching products, and serving paid content to buyers. It is never used as a standalone template; other templates absorb its actions and HTML definitions by listing `base-checkout` in their `extends` array (see `Template.Inherit` in `model/template.go`, which copies actions, states, roles, and parsed HTML templates from the parent unless the child overrides them). No embedded template currently extends it — its consumer today is the external Bandwagon package, whose `bandwagon-album` template extends it to sell albums.

The actions in [template.hjson](template.hjson) split into two audiences. For authors/editors, `edit-checkout` renders [edit-checkout.html](edit-checkout.html), a three-step "Sell Online" wizard (merchant account, products, content) driven by the Stream's MerchantAccounts and Products, and `edit-products` saves product selections through the `set-privileges` step. For anonymous visitors, `checkout` forwards straight to `/.checkout?productId=...` when the Stream has exactly one attached product, and otherwise opens [checkout.html](checkout.html) as a modal product picker; `paid-content` renders [paid-content.html](paid-content.html), which lists the Stream's attachments in the "paid" category for buyers. [edit-paid-content.html](edit-paid-content.html) is a deliberate stub — extending templates are expected to override it with their own paid-content upload UI.

## Additional Information

### Seller setup: the edit-checkout wizard

[edit-checkout.html](edit-checkout.html) branches on `.MerchantAccounts` ([build/builder_stream.go](../../../build/builder_stream.go)), a query over the MerchantAccounts owned by the Stream's author. When the author has none, it renders step 1 only: provider cards from `.AvailableMerchantAccounts` ([build/builder_common.go](../../../build/builder_common.go)), each linking to `/.{provider}/connect` — for Stripe Connect that is `GET /.stripe-connect/connect` → `handler.GetStripeConnect` in [server.go](../../../server.go) — plus a `/@me/settings/merchantAccount-add` modal. Once at least one account exists, the page becomes a three-tab wizard (hyperscript `TabContainer` behavior) whose progress is derived from `.StreamHasPrivileges`: the Products tab lazy-loads `GET /{streamId}/edit-products` via `hx-trigger="load"`, and the Content tab inlines the `edit-paid-content` view with `{{.View "edit-paid-content"}}`. The page also listens for `refreshPage from:window` and re-fetches itself into `main`, which is how the `refresh-page` step at the end of `edit-products` redraws the wizard after a save.

The `edit-products` action is where products are attached to the Stream. Its `set-privileges` step ([build/step_SetPrivileges.go](../../../build/step_SetPrivileges.go)) does double duty: on GET it calls `factory.Product().SyncRemoteProducts` to pull the author's current products/prices from the merchant account's API and renders a role-based multi-select of the author's Circles and Products; on POST it clears and rewrites `stream.Circles` and `stream.Products`, parsing form keys of the form `circles.{role}` / `products.{role}` into ObjectID slices keyed by role. The following `save` step persists the Stream, and the Stream's product IDs then surface through `.PrivilegeIDs` (a `model.Permissions`, [build/builder_stream.go](../../../build/builder_stream.go)).

### Buyer checkout: /.checkout and back

The anonymous `checkout` action forwards to `GET /.checkout?productId=...&return=...` (directly when `.PrivilegeIDs` has exactly one entry, otherwise after the [checkout.html](checkout.html) product-picker modal). That route is wrapped in `WithProduct` middleware and handled by `GetCheckout` in [handler/checkout.go](../../../handler/checkout.go), which locks the customer email to a signed-in guest Identity's verified address (anonymous buyers enter their own at Stripe), then asks `MerchantAccount.GetCheckoutURL` ([service/merchantAccount_.go](../../../service/merchantAccount_.go)) for a hosted checkout URL and 307-redirects there — only `ConnectionProviderStripeConnect` is live in that switch; the direct-Stripe and PayPal cases are commented out. After payment, Stripe returns the buyer to `GET /.checkout/response` (`WithMerchantAccountJWT` middleware), where `GetCheckoutResponse` verifies the transaction via `ParseCheckoutResponse` and records a Privilege attached to the buyer's email Identity. A buyer already signed in as that Identity is redirected to `/@guest`; anyone else is sent a one-time guest-code email and shown the `checkout-claim` domain template, because Stripe does not verify customer emails and granting a session outright would allow account takeover. Ongoing subscription changes for purchased Privileges arrive separately at `POST /.stripe-connect/webhook/checkout` ([server.go](../../../server.go)), and the purchased files themselves are served by this template's `paid-content` action from the Stream's "paid" attachment category.
