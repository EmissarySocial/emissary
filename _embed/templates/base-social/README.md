# Base Social

Shared base template (category: Base, model: None) that supplies the standard social-interaction controls — reply, share (Announce), and like buttons — plus small partials for rendering hashtags and rule-label warning chips. Templates absorb these by listing `base-social` in their `extends` array; embedded consumers are `user-inbox`, `user-outbox`, `user-notifications`, and `stream-outbox-message`, and the external Bandwagon templates extend it as well.

The interactive actions in [template.hjson](template.hjson) all follow the same htmx pattern: the button posts back to its own action with `hx-vals` describing the response, the `set-response` step records or removes the viewer's Like/Announce, and `view-html` (with `method: both`) re-renders the button in its new state via `hx-swap="outerHTML"`. [like-button.html](like-button.html) is the authenticated Share/Like pair, [btn-social-outline.html](btn-social-outline.html) is the outline-styled variant that also shows a Reply button, and [reply-button.html](reply-button.html) links into `/@me/newsfeed/browse-reply`. The remaining files are pure partials with no action of their own, invoked by name from consuming templates: [label-chips.html](label-chips.html) renders a LabelSet as warning chips (USER-tier labels link to the rule that caused them, ADMIN-tier labels are text-only), [tags.html](tags.html) renders label annotations followed by a document's hashtags, and [hidden-placeholder.html](hidden-placeholder.html) renders the collapsed "Select to show" state for documents hidden by the viewer's rules.
