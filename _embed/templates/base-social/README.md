# Base Social

Shared base template (category: Base, model: None) that supplies the standard social-interaction controls — reply, boost (Announce), and like buttons — plus small partials for rendering hashtags and rule-label warning chips. Templates absorb these by listing `base-social` in their `extends` array; embedded consumers are `user-inbox`, `user-outbox`, `user-notifications`, and `stream-outbox-message`, and the external Bandwagon templates extend it as well.

The interactive actions in [template.hjson](template.hjson) all follow the same htmx pattern: the button posts back to its own action with `hx-vals` describing the response, the `set-response` step records or removes the viewer's Like/Announce, and `view-html` (with `method: both`) re-renders the button in its new state via `hx-swap="outerHTML"`. [like-button.html](like-button.html) is the authenticated Boost/Like pair, [btn-social-outline.html](btn-social-outline.html) is the outline-styled variant that also shows Reply and ends with Share, and [reply-button.html](reply-button.html) links into `/@me/newsfeed/browse-reply`. [share-button.html](share-button.html) takes the URL to share and opens the OS share menu through theme-global's `Share` behavior, which shows it only where the browser has one. It is never part of `like-button`, because the inbox puts Share after its Notes button, and it stays inside `btn-social-outline`'s swapped span so that a Like or Boost cannot copy it. The remaining files are pure partials with no action of their own, invoked by name from consuming templates: [label-chips.html](label-chips.html) renders a LabelSet as warning chips (USER-tier labels link to the rule that caused them, ADMIN-tier labels are text-only), [tags.html](tags.html) renders label annotations followed by a document's hashtags, and [hidden-placeholder.html](hidden-placeholder.html) renders the collapsed "Select to show" state for documents hidden by the viewer's rules.

[reply-button.html](reply-button.html) takes `(array $builder $url)`, not a bare URL, and draws nothing when the post's own reply policy refuses the viewer. It calls `$builder.CanReplyTo`, which only the Inbox builder has, so every caller is an inbox template (`user-inbox` and qwertylicious' `qwerty-inbox`), rendered by the Inbox builder itself and never from inside a `with-*` step, whose builder is the record's `Model`. The policy is the post's GoToSocial `interactionPolicy`, read by hannibal's `InteractionPolicy.CanInteract` (emissary-specs REPLY-CONTROLS §1.8).
